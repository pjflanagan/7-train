
# Appearance and Rename

We're going to call this app "7 Train". The theme is going to be MTA.

## Header Logo

We can make the header logo as an SVG. It should be a 7 on a purple circle.

## Theme

- [x] The default colors need to exactly match the MTA colors.
- [x] The backgrounds will be black #1c1c1c.
- [x] The fond it Helvetica

- [ ] No rounded corners for signs, all sharp.
- [ ] The header should be dark green vertical bars like a subway entrance, with a black rectangle on it at the center like the subway sign.
- [ ] The week will not be a card anymore. The "This week" title will be graffiti directly on the background. Progress is on a "TV screen." Dnd workout options are on "signs."
- [ ] The day cards will also be "TV screens". The workout types will be colored circles connected by white lines like a subway map

- [ ] The MTA also is famous for its arrows. We will mimic those for things like external links and dropdowns.

- [ ] The footer is the yellow dot pattern on the entrance, text can be "trash" thrown on the ground.

- [ ] Modals should be silver like a subway car and slightly sweep in from the right and and head to the left on close

- [ ] The loading modal should be a metrocard swipe animation, it heads to the left and when it is all the way across we are loaded
